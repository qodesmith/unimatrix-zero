# Onboarding and sign-in flow: what the user likes

Running list from reviewing the prototype. Feeds the resolution of the Onboarding and sign-in flow ticket.

**Base: variant C (Connections hub).** It puts the user straight at the decision to make: which AI to connect.

1. **A's intro sentence on the hub.** First launch opens with "Talk with Claude or ChatGPT, and branch any conversation into as many directions as you like. It uses the plan you already pay for." above "Connect the AI you already pay for".
2. **One connect at a time.** While one Provider is connecting (download through plan check), the other card's Connect / Try again button is disabled, with "You can connect X once Y is done." It unlocks when the first connects, fails or is cancelled.
3. **Warn about the vendor's wording.** While waiting on the sign-in page, the card says "Claude's page may say “Claude Code”. That's normal." (or "Codex" for ChatGPT), because the vendor's unmodified program runs the sign-in.
4. **The download wait is fine.** The vendor program must finish downloading before its sign-in page can open; "Get Claude ready" with a progress bar covers it.
5. **Settings placement is not this ticket.** Connecting a second Provider later happens from Settings, reached from a settings entry or gear in the app's left sidebar. Where that lives belongs with Settings and the sidebar.
