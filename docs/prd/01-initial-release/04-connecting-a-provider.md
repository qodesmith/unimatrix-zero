# 4. Connecting a Provider

Source: [Onboarding and sign-in flow](https://github.com/qodesmith/unimatrix-zero/issues/16) (prototype, variant C), [Can sign-in and CLI install be fully hidden?](https://github.com/qodesmith/unimatrix-zero/issues/4), [Ship the vendor programs or use the user's installed copies](https://github.com/qodesmith/unimatrix-zero/issues/20).

- **First launch is one Connections screen.** An intro line ("Talk with Claude or ChatGPT, and branch any chat into as many directions as you like. It uses the plan you already pay for."), then Claude and ChatGPT side by side as cards (logo, name, "Pro or Max · by Anthropic" / "Plus or Pro · by OpenAI", Connect). "Start chatting →" unlocks once one Provider is connected and opens the empty Tree.
- **Connecting a card** turns it into a checklist: **Get Claude ready** (download with progress, "24 of 100 MB") → **Sign in on Claude's page** (opens the vendor's own page in the browser, with "Claude's page may say "Claude Code". That's normal." and "Don't see the page? Open it again") → **Paste the code** (Claude only, when its page shows one) → **Check your plan**. Cancel is available throughout. One Provider connects at a time. Done: a green card with "Connected", the email and the plan.
- **Mechanics.** The vendor program is downloaded on Connect ([ADR 0008](../../adr/0008-app-managed-vendor-programs.md)), so the sign-in page can only open once the download finishes. Claude signs in through the unmodified `claude auth login` with no TTY (the app shows the URL and a paste-code field); ChatGPT through `codex app-server`'s `account/login/start` and the `account/login/completed` notification. Status, email and plan come from `claude auth status` / the SDK's `accountInfo()` and Codex `account/read`. The app never sees or stores a credential. `claude setup-token` is rejected, even as an opt-in.
- **Errors** show on the card in plain words with one action: download failed or offline ("Couldn't get Claude ready. Check your internet connection and try again." → Try again); cancelled or no answer on the vendor page (→ Try again); wrong code (inline); a Claude Free account ("Unimatrix Zero needs Claude Pro or Max." → Use a different account).
- **Later:** the second Provider is connected from Settings → Connections, which shows the same cards. Sign out and Remove are in [§18](./18-offline-reconnect-and-error-states.md).
